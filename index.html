How it works

Tune in. Speak up. Make a show.

Live

Listen

The bRadio Lounge is a public room on bChatX with live audio. When a show is on, open the link in any browser and listen. Follow it to get a ping the next time it goes live.

Live

Raise your hand

Sign in with bWalletX, raise your hand, and the host can bring you on stage to talk. Chat alongside the show while it runs.

Live

Record a show

Bitcoin Radio is a studio in the browser: sign in with bWalletX, record, and save your recording on-chain from your own wallet.

The X in bRadioX

A radio exchange, coming.

The X stands for exchange. Next, shows and artists get their own tokens and rooms, so listeners can back the shows they want more of and join the room behind each one. It isn't live yet; it moves to Live in the list below when it is.
